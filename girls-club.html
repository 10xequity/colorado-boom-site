Girls Club Volleyball

Ages 10U–18U  |  Season: November – June  |  Sanctioned by RMR and USAV

Girls Program Overview

Competitive levels for 2026–27 are set by age. 11U–14U teams play Colorado (in-state RMR plus one committed travel event) or National (offered at 14U only, two committed events). 15U–18U teams play Regional (no committed travel), Colorado (two committed events), or National (three). Committed travel and fees rise with the level; the Travel Fee covers coaches’ travel and tournament fees only — family travel is separate. RYL is included for 14U-and-under teams (Sep–Oct for players not in high school volleyball). See the full breakdown in the Club Fees section. Players compete from November through June at tournaments across Colorado and beyond.

More Touches, More Growth

Practice: 5:00–7:30 PM Three days a week (M/W/F or T/Th/F) · one location, all season
  • Home for dinner. Every practice wraps by 7:30 PM — no late-night sessions.
  • Families and players first. Predictable times and one location you can plan your life around.
  • 2.5-hour sessions, 3 days/week (M/W/F or T/Th/F).
  • Integrated 30-minute conditioning block at the end of practice.
  • Friday: dedicated scrimmage and position-specific sessions.
  • One location always — no bouncing between facilities.
  • Makeup practices on alternate days.
  • Multi-sport athletes can reschedule to attend other days' practices.
  • Boomtown drop-in included for all club members.
