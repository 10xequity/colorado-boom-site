Hiring Club Coaches

We're hiring for the season starting in November: head coaches, assistant coaches, and trainers across multiple age groups, on travel and regional teams. The positions are paid, and the schedule is built to work around a day job.

Apply Now See the Roles

Role Descriptions

Three ways to coach with us this season.

Head Coach

One to two openings. You lead a team's season: practices, lineups, and match day. Travel and regional teams are both available, so tell us which fits you.

Assistant Coach

Six openings across age groups. You support the head coach, run drills, and help players develop. A good way in for former players who want to start coaching.

Trainer

Run skills sessions and clinics, from position work to developmental training. Good for coaches who want to teach without a full team schedule.

What You Get

Competitive Pay

Head and assistant positions are paid, and team gear is included.

Non-Travel Teams

Choose a travel team or stay local. Coach at the level of commitment that fits your life.

Mentorship

Learn from coaches with national tournament experience.

Common Questions
