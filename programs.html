The Sessions, Explained

Every session below is included in the one membership. Pick the days that fit.

Littles: Little Spikers

Ages 6–9 · Tues / Thurs · 5:00–6:30 PM

Less screen time, more play time. A first volleyball program built for little kids: encouraging, safe and fun, with the ball in their hands the whole session. Movement, catching and tossing, underhand serving and the very first passes, taught in small groups by Boom coaches. No experience needed.

Developmental Skills & Mechanics

Ages 8–16 · Tues / Thurs · 5:00–6:30 PM

Foundational skills and biomechanics: passing, setting, serving, and court movement, taught in a supportive, small-group setting. Built for newer and younger players, and a clear pathway into club volleyball.

Advanced Skills & Mechanics

Players 14+ · Tues / Wed / Thurs · 5:00–7:00 PM

Higher-rep, position-specific technique and mechanics for committed players 14 and up. Sharpens form, footwork, and volleyball IQ for club-level competition.

Advanced Club Training

Tues / Thurs · 5:00–7:00 PM

High-rep, competitive training for committed players preparing for the club season. The most game-like reps of the week.

Girls League

Girls 14U–18U  |  Aug–Oct  |  $80 for 8 weeks

A flexible, competitive league for girls 14U–18U who want steady reps outside the club season. Eight weeks of guided match play and skill work at Boomtown Fieldhouse. A great way to stay sharp between school ball and club, with no full-season commitment. Open to any Colorado Boom club player.

Rocky Mountain Youth League (RYL)

Girls 14U & under  |  Season: Sep–Oct  |  Five league events  |  $500

The RYL is the Rocky Mountain Region’s fall youth league, the stepping stone between developmental volleyball and full club play. It mirrors the club experience with real rules, real competition, and real teamwork, without the time and cost of a full club season. Four one-day Sunday tournaments plus a two-day championship weekend run September and October at participating clubs across the Denver metro.

At Colorado Boom, RYL teams train in a true club environment: club coaches and two team practices per week, all at Boomtown Fieldhouse (days for each team are on the RYL Teams page). RYL is included for our rostered 14U & under club players. We also field RYL-only teams for players who want a season of competitive volleyball before committing to club. The RYL-only season is $500.

Tryouts are combined: our girls’ club tryouts (10U–18U) run Sun Oct 11at any Wed / Fri open gym, and one tryout covers both pathways. New to USAV/RMR? You’ll only need the low-cost $20 RYL membership ($15 of it applies toward a full membership if you later join club).
